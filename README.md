## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Problem: "The application won't start after installation"**  
**Solution:** Right-click on the AutoHub CRM icon and select "Run as administrator." This usually resolves any permission issues. If it still doesn't work, restart your computer and try again.

**Problem: "I can't see my vehicle inventory"**  
**Solution:** Check your internet connection. AutoHub CRM syncs data to the cloud for backup, but it requires an active connection to load your information. After reconnecting, click the "Refresh" button.

**Problem: "The interface looks small on my screen"**  
**Solution:** Hold down the "Ctrl" key and scroll your mouse wheel up to zoom in. Alternatively, adjust your Windows display settings to 125% or 150% scaling.

**Problem: "I forgot my password"**  
**Solution:** On the login screen, click "Forgot Password." Enter the email address you registered with, and a password reset link will be sent to you within minutes.
